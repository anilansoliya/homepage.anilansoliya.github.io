Who I Am
About Me

I am a Ph.D. researcher in Mechanical Engineering at PDPM IIITDM Jabalpur, working under the supervision of Prof. Vijay Kumar Gupta. My doctoral thesis focuses on Real-Time Fault Detection in FFF 3D Printing Using Machine Learning.

My research bridges the gap between physical manufacturing systems and intelligent data-driven models — designing multimodal sensor systems, building supervised ML pipelines, and creating real-time anomaly detection frameworks that automatically halt defective prints to reduce material waste.

Currently, I am also serving as a Junior Research Fellow (JRF) at MANIT Bhopal, where I work on ML-assisted multiscale modeling of piezoelectric composite materials using COMSOL and XGBoost.
